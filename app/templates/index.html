Atoms Call Ledger

Loss-proof webhook ingestion & deadline-aware reconciliation for Smallest.ai Atoms voice calls.

The Core Problem

1. No Platform Retries

Atoms webhooks do not retry on 5xx or network drops. Failed deliveries are logged FAILED and the event is dropped forever.

2. Raw-Byte HMAC

Authenticity relies entirely on X-Signature computed over raw request bytes. Re-serializing JSON breaks the signature.

3. ZDR Deletion Window

Under Enterprise Zero Data Retention (1h to 30d), call records are irrevocably deleted once the window elapses. Webhooks are your copy.

Five Confirmed Operational Realities

  • CONFIRMED FACT Three events fire per call (pre-conversation, post-conversation, analytics-completed), joined by callId.
  • CONFIRMED FACT Delivery timeout is 30s. Atoms does not retry failed deliveries (official docs).
  • CONFIRMED FACT No static egress IPs exist; authenticity depends on X-Signature HMAC-SHA256 over raw unparsed request bytes.
  • REASONABLE INFERENCE No timestamp exists in the signature; replay protection must be handled at the application layer via idempotency on (callId, eventType).
  • CONFIRMED FACT Under Zero Data Retention, unrecovered transcripts and recordings cannot be recovered once the retention window expires.
